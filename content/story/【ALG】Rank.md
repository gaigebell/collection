#### The Row Space

${\rm Row\;} A = {\rm Col\;} A^T$


#### The Rank Theorem

##### Definition

>[!hint] Definition
>The rank of $A$ is the dimension of the column space of $A$
>$r(A) = {\rm dim}\; A$

##### Theorem 14

>[!hint] The Rank Theorem
>$r(A) + {\rm dim}\;{\rm Nul}\; A = n$


number of pivot columns + number of non-pivot columns = number of columns


### Rank and the Invertible Matrix Theorem

##### Theorem
[[【ALG】The Inverse of a Matrix]]

> [!hint]
> - The columns of $A$ form a basis of ${\mathbb R}^n$
> - ${\rm Col} A = {\mathbb R}^n$
> - ${\rm dim}\;{\rm Col}\; A = n$
> - $rank(A) = n$
> - ${\rm Nul} A = \{{\mathbf 0}\}$
> - ${\rm dim}\; {\rm Nul}\; A = 0$
